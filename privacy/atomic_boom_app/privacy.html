Atomic Boom - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atomic Boom, an educational chemistry game, collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the app. The app is provided by 67.603.178 LUCAS CAMPOS ACHCAR ("we", "us", or "our").

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or precise location to play Atomic Boom. We do not intentionally collect information that directly identifies you.

When enabled in the app, Google Firebase services may automatically collect technical and gameplay information necessary to operate, understand, and improve the application. This may include:

  • Analytics: app interactions, session duration, game mode usage, progression, score events, feature usage, performance metrics, app version, device type, operating system version, and language.
  • Crash reports: crash logs, diagnostic information, and app stability data through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Push notifications: a device or messaging token and notification delivery information through Firebase Cloud Messaging, when notifications are enabled.
  • Advertising: the app may display advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and advertising interaction information in accordance with their policies and your available consent choices.
  • Leaderboards: if you choose to use Google Play Games features, Google may process your Play Games profile and game scores to provide rankings.

We use this information to provide and maintain game features, deliver notifications, show and measure advertising, analyze usage, diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We share or make information available only to service providers that help us operate Atomic Boom. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: app usage analysis and crash diagnostics.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): push notification delivery.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Play Games: optional leaderboard functionality.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain technical, analytics, crash, messaging, and advertising information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods may also be determined by the relevant service provider.

Atomic Boom does not provide an account system. To request deletion of information associated with your use of the app, email lc.achcar.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Atomic Boom Data Deletion Request". We will review and process the request as required by applicable law and explain any information that cannot be deleted.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information handled by Atomic Boom. Data transmitted to third-party services is protected using the security features provided by those services. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Atomic Boom is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate. Advertising and other third-party services may apply their own age and consent controls.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We recommend that you review this page regularly to be aware of any changes.
