Atomic Boom - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atomic Boom collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the app. The app is provided by 67.603.178 LUCAS CAMPOS ACHCAR ("we", "us", or "our").

Information We Collect

Atomic Boom does not require you to create an account with us or provide an email address, phone number, or postal address to play in Guest mode. The app also offers optional sign-in through Google Play Games. If you use this feature, the app accesses your Google Play Games player display name (Gamertag) to show it in the game. Google manages your Google Account and Google Play Games profile.

Third-party services integrated into Atomic Boom may collect and process technical, usage, advertising, diagnostic, messaging, profile, and gameplay information when their features are used or enabled. Depending on the service, your device settings, your region, and your consent choices, this may include:

  • Firebase Analytics: app interactions, session information, game mode usage, progression, score events, feature usage, app version, device type, operating system version, language, and app or installation identifiers.
  • Crash reports: if Firebase Crashlytics is integrated and enabled, it may process crash logs, diagnostic information, and app stability data.
  • Push notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a messaging registration token and technical delivery information to deliver push notifications.
  • Advertising: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad requests, and advertising interaction information for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention, subject to applicable settings and consent choices.
  • Google Play Games: if you choose to sign in or use leaderboards, Google may process your player profile information, including your player display name, and game scores to provide sign-in and ranking features.

We use the features and information made available through these services to operate and improve Atomic Boom, deliver requested notifications, provide Google Play Games features, display and measure advertisements, understand app usage, diagnose technical issues when applicable, and help prevent abuse. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Information handled by the integrated services may be transmitted to and processed by Google and its relevant service providers to provide the features described in this policy. The processing of that information is subject to the applicable terms, settings, and privacy information of those services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: app usage analysis and crash diagnostics.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): push notification delivery.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Play Games: optional leaderboard functionality.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, the Firebase privacy information, and the AdMob information.

Data Retention

Atomic Boom does not operate its own user account system or maintain a separate Google Account or Google Play Games profile for users. Information processed by Firebase, AdMob, and Google Play Games may be retained by those providers according to their respective retention policies, settings, and applicable legal requirements. We do not control the retention periods for information stored in Google's systems.

Data Deletion Requests

Atomic Boom does not provide its own user account system or an in-app account deletion feature. If you believe that we hold information about you, or you would like us to review a privacy or deletion request concerning information under our control, contact us at achcarlucas@lcadev.com.br with the subject "Atomic Boom - Data Deletion Request".

We will review the request and, where applicable, delete information held by us that we can identify and delete, subject to legal requirements. We cannot directly delete information held in Google's systems. To manage or request deletion of information associated with your Google Account or Google Play Games profile, use the privacy and account controls provided by Google. For information processed by Firebase or AdMob, consult the relevant Google service settings and privacy resources.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information handled by Atomic Boom. Information transmitted to Google services is protected using the security measures provided by those services. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Atomic Boom is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The app does not ask Guest users to provide personal contact details. Third-party services integrated into the app may process device or usage information and may apply their own age, consent, and privacy controls. If you have a concern about information relating to a child, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We recommend that you review this page regularly to be aware of any changes.
