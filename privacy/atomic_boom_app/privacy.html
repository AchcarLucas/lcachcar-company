Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our educational chemistry game collects, uses, and protects user information.

Data Collection

We do not require user authentication in the game. The game does not request or store personal information such as names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, precise location, profile data, or any other personally identifiable information.

We use Google Firebase services to collect only technical and gameplay data necessary to improve the application. Specifically:

  • Analytics: We collect non-personal usage data such as session duration, game mode usage, progression, score events, feature usage, performance metrics, app version, device type, operating system version, and language. This data is used only to understand how the game is played and to improve gameplay and stability.
  • Crashlytics: We collect crash and error information to diagnose stability issues and improve the reliability of the game.
  • Messaging: We may use push notifications to send informational or important messages related to the game. We do not use this service to collect personal user data.

We do not use Firebase or any other service to collect personal user data, private messages, account information, or identity-related information. The data collected is limited to game and software activity and is not used to identify a specific individual.

Third-Party Services

To support the game's functionality, we use the following third-party services, which process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Analytics: Used to measure aggregated gameplay and app usage patterns for product improvement.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Used to collect crash reports and app stability diagnostics.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to send push notifications when necessary.
  • Google Play Games (Leaderboard): We may use the Google Play Games leaderboard system to allow users to view rankings. Google Play Games may collect data necessary for managing this functionality, in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

As a game aimed at high school students, we are committed to protecting the privacy of all our users. No personal data is intentionally collected for profiling, advertising personalization, or user identification. The data used through Firebase is limited to non-personal game and software metrics necessary to improve the experience and maintain the stability of the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We recommend that you review this page regularly to be aware of any changes.

Contact
