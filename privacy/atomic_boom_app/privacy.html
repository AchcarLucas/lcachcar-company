This Privacy Policy describes how our educational chemistry game collects, uses, and protects user information.

We do not collect, store, or process any personal user data. The game does not request information such as names, emails, locations, or any other personally identifiable information.

To support the game's functionality, we utilize the following third-party services which may process data according to their own privacy policies:

Children's Privacy

As a game aimed at high school students, we are committed to protecting the privacy of all our users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We recommend that you review this page regularly to be aware of any changes.

Contact
